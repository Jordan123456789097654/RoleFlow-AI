# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]
**Last updated:** [DATE]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out what you may and may not do with RoleFlow, including the chat application, artifacts and live previews, the API, and related services (the "Services"). It is part of our Terms of Service. Violations may result in content being blocked, held, or removed; feature limits; suspension or termination; and, where required by law, reports to authorities.

Our goal is to keep RoleFlow useful and safe for the people who use it. Most people use the Services for ordinary and legitimate purposes, and this policy is written to make clear where the lines are.

## 1. Principles

- **Lawfulness.** Do not use the Services for any activity that violates applicable law.
- **Respect.** Do not use the Services to harass, threaten, defame, or exploit people.
- **Safety.** Do not use the Services to create content or capabilities that put people at risk of serious harm.
- **Integrity.** Do not use the Services to deceive people in ways that cause harm, or to undermine systems that protect people.
- **Responsibility.** You are accountable for what you prompt, what you share, and what you do with output.

## 2. Permitted and Encouraged Uses

You are welcome to use RoleFlow for:

- answering questions, research, learning, and explanations;
- writing, editing, translating, summarizing, brainstorming, and planning;
- software development, debugging, code review, and building prototypes and tools;
- analysis of data, documents, and images you have the right to use;
- creative fiction, poetry, role-play, and worldbuilding that does not cross the lines in Section 3;
- education, accessibility, and professional productivity;
- security education and defensive work conducted lawfully on systems you own or are authorized to test, subject to Section 4.

## 3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

### 3.1 Child safety (zero tolerance)

You must not create, request, solicit, distribute, or possess content that sexualizes minors, including text, images, audio, video, or fictional depictions in sexual contexts. You must not use the Services to groom, exploit, or traffic minors, to obtain sexual content involving minors, or to describe sexual contact with a minor. Any attempt to do so will result in immediate and permanent termination of the account, and we will report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children and to law enforcement as required by law.

We will not generate content that sexualizes any person under 18, real or fictional, and we will not produce content that depicts minors in a sexual manner regardless of stated age, framing, or purpose.

### 3.2 Violence, weapons, and mass harm

You must not use the Services to:

- plan, encourage, glorify, or facilitate violence against a person or group, including threats of harm to a specific person;
- make, acquire, or improve explosives, incendiary devices, chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear weapons, or toxins intended to cause harm;
- manufacture or convert firearms or firearm components in ways that violate law, such as creating fully automatic conversions or untraceable firearms;
- plan attacks on infrastructure, public spaces, schools, places of worship, transportation, government buildings, or critical systems;
- plan or promote mass violence, including active shooter tactics intended to increase casualties.

General discussion of history, policy, journalism, fiction that does not give operational detail, and safety information is not prohibited by this section.

### 3.3 Violent extremism and terrorism

You must not use the Services to recruit for, fundraise for, promote, or provide material support to terrorist or violent extremist organizations; to produce propaganda or manifestos; to celebrate perpetrators of terrorist attacks; or to incite violence based on race, religion, national origin, sex, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or other protected characteristics.

### 3.4 Harassment, threats, and abuse

You must not use the Services to:

- threaten, intimidate, or stalk any person;
- create content that targets a specific individual for harassment or doxxing, including compiling private addresses, phone numbers, workplaces, or daily routines with intent to harm;
- generate sexually explicit content depicting a real person without their consent, including "deepfake" intimate imagery;
- produce content designed to humiliate, degrade, or dehumanize people based on protected characteristics;
- create or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery of any kind.

### 3.5 Self-harm and suicide

You must not use the Services to encourage, plan, or instruct self-harm or suicide, to obtain methods, lethal doses, or means, or to create content that glamorizes these acts. If you or someone you know is in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line. In the United States, you can call or text 988. Our assistant will try to provide support and resources in these situations.

### 3.6 Eating disorders

You must not use the Services to obtain content that promotes or instructs disordered eating, including extreme caloric restriction, purging, or compensatory behavior, or that encourages the pursuit of an unhealthy body.

### 3.7 Illegal drugs

You must not use the Services to obtain instructions for synthesizing, cultivating, or producing controlled substances, or to plan drug trafficking. General harm-reduction and safety information is permitted.

### 3.8 Fraud, scams, and deception

You must not use the Services to:

- create phishing messages, fake login pages, scam scripts, or impersonation campaigns;
- steal credentials, payment information, or personal data;
- create fake reviews, astroturfing campaigns, or coordinated inauthentic behavior;
- forge documents, identification, credentials, or signatures;
- manipulate markets, commit securities fraud, or engage in other financial crimes;
- generate disinformation intended to deceive voters about voting procedures, dates, or eligibility.

### 3.9 Privacy and surveillance

You must not use the Services to:

- surveil, track, or monitor individuals without their knowledge or lawful authority;
- uncover private information about people for stalking, harassment, or discrimination;
- build tools that scrape or harvest personal data in violation of law or of platform terms;
- process another person's sensitive data (health, financial, biometric, or identifying data) without a lawful basis.

### 3.10 Impersonation and misrepresentation

You must not impersonate another person or organization in a way that is intended to deceive, including by posing as a government official, a law-enforcement officer, a medical professional, or a company representative. Parody and clearly labeled fiction are permitted where they are not used to deceive.

### 3.11 Intellectual property

You must not use the Services to infringe copyrights, trademarks, or other intellectual property. This includes reproducing large portions of copyrighted works, circumventing digital rights management, or distributing pirated material.

### 3.12 Regulated professional advice

You may ask for general information about medical, legal, financial, and tax topics. You must not represent output as the advice of a licensed professional, and you must not use the Services to provide individualized professional services to others in a regulated capacity without appropriate qualification and oversight.

## 4. Cybersecurity and Software

You must not use the Services to create, improve, or deploy:

- malware, ransomware, keyloggers, remote access trojans, rootkits, worms, or viruses;
- exploits or payloads intended to attack systems you do not own or have authorization to test;
- denial-of-service tools, botnets, or attack infrastructure;
- credential-stuffing tools, brute-force cracking tools against accounts you do not control, or automated account takeover tools;
- tools designed to evade security controls to conduct unauthorized access or exfiltration of data.

Discussion of security concepts, defensive engineering, vulnerability education, and code review that does not provide operational attack capability is permitted. We may restrict specific cybersecurity capabilities in our Services at our discretion.

## 5. Platform and API Use

You must not:

- circumvent usage limits, rate limits, or access controls, including by creating multiple accounts;
- share or sell API keys, or allow others to use your key outside your organization without authorization;
- use the Services to build a competing model by systematically extracting outputs for training, except as expressly permitted in writing;
- reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to discover the source code, model weights, or safety systems of the Services, except where permitted by law;
- interfere with, overload, or probe the security of the Services, including through automated scanning without written permission;
- use the Services to generate spam, bulk unsolicited messages, or content intended to manipulate search or social platforms;
- misrepresent the source of output or claim output is human-written where that is material to others and prohibited by their rules.

## 6. Generated Code and Artifacts

- Artifacts and live previews run in a restricted sandbox. You must not attempt to escape the sandbox, exfiltrate data from it, or use it to mine cryptocurrency or run unauthorized network scans.
- Do not publish, distribute, or deploy generated code that violates this AUP.
- Test and review code before relying on it, particularly code that handles payments, authentication, personal data, or health information.

## 7. Uploads and Attachments

You must not upload content that you do not have the right to upload, content that violates this AUP, or files intended to exploit the Services. Images and documents are subject to automated safety review. Images that cannot be reviewed automatically may be held until they can be checked. Attachments may be scanned for malware and for policy violations.

## 8. Shared Conversations

When you create a public share link, you are responsible for ensuring the shared content complies with this policy and does not expose other people's private information. We may disable share links that violate this policy.

## 9. Reporting Violations

If you see content or behavior that violates this policy, report it to support@roleflow.site with the subject line "AUP Violation Report." Include the relevant link, the time, and a description. Do not include unnecessary personal information about others. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

For content involving child sexual abuse material, do not share or forward the material. Report it to us and to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children at report.cybertip.org, or to local law enforcement.

## 10. Enforcement

We take enforcement actions that are proportionate to the seriousness of the violation, the harm involved, and your history with the Services. Actions may include:

1. a warning and content removal;
2. blocking the specific request, with an explanation of the category;
3. temporary restriction of features, rate limits, or API access;
4. suspension of your account or key;
5. permanent termination of your account;
6. reporting to authorities and cooperation with lawful requests.

Some violations, particularly in Section 3.1, 3.2, and 3.3, may result in immediate termination without a prior warning. Enforcement decisions, appeals, and the review process are described in our Moderation and Appeals Policy.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify account holders in advance where practicable.

## 12. Questions

Questions about this policy may be sent to support@roleflow.site.
