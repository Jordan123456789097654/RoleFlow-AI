# AI Output and Content Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]

This policy explains how RoleFlow's AI-generated output works, what you should and should not rely on it for, and how we handle content and feedback. It supplements the Terms of Service.

## 1. What Output Is

RoleFlow produces text, code, explanations, and other content in response to your inputs using large language models that we access through third-party providers. Output is generated probabilistically, which means the same input may produce different results, and it does not reflect a verified database of facts.

## 2. Accuracy and Verification

- Output may be wrong, outdated, biased, or incomplete, even when it sounds certain.
- Citations, quotations, statistics, case names, URLs, package names, and API signatures may be fabricated. Verify them independently.
- For high-stakes decisions in medicine, law, finance, taxes, employment, safety engineering, or security, consult a qualified professional.
- Do not rely on output as the only basis for decisions that affect someone's health, safety, rights, or money.

## 3. Code and Artifacts

- Review generated code before running it. It may contain bugs, insecure patterns, licensing issues, or dependencies that do not exist.
- Live previews run in a sandboxed iframe. Downloaded files are not sandboxed once you save them, so treat downloaded code as untrusted until reviewed.
- Do not paste secrets, credentials, or production keys into prompts, and do not ask the assistant to store them.

## 4. Ownership and Use

Subject to the Terms of Service, you may use output for lawful purposes, including commercial purposes. You are responsible for ensuring your use does not infringe third-party rights, violate law, or breach contracts or platform rules. Output may resemble existing material, and we do not guarantee that output is unique.

## 5. Disclosure

Where you publish, submit, or distribute AI-generated content in contexts that require disclosure, such as academic work, regulated communications, or political advertising, you are responsible for complying with those requirements, including disclosing AI assistance where required.

## 6. Feedback

The thumbs up and thumbs down buttons send your rating and the associated exchange to us so we can evaluate and improve the Services. Do not include personal information in feedback you give us unless necessary. Feedback can be shared with our providers only as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Limitations on Output

The Services will not generate content that violates the Acceptable Use Policy. Where a request falls into a restricted category, the assistant may decline, provide general information instead, or direct you to appropriate resources. Refusals are not a judgment of your character.

## 8. Changes

We may improve models, switch providers, or change behavior. Output may change over time.
